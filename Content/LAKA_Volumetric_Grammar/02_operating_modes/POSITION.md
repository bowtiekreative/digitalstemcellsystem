# POSITION — LAKA Operating Playbook

**Status:** Implementation extension to the conversation’s six-mode architecture. This is a procedure to apply, not an already functioning software tool.

## Purpose

Define a meaningful and supportable market position for a specified audience and Scenario.

## Inputs

Audience, customer Outcomes, current alternatives, dated competitor evidence, business capabilities, delivery costs, and evidence of demand.

## Procedure

1. Choose a customer Scenario and separate users, beneficiaries, deciders, payers, and providers where they differ.
2. Compare alternatives using the same definitions, evidence period, and Outcome measures.
3. Map customer Importance and Satisfaction without substituting the analyst’s guesses for customer responses.
4. Identify candidate under-served or over-served Outcomes and distinguish an unstudied gap from an actual unmet need.
5. Compose a position around the beneficiary, Outcome, mechanism, and demonstrated difference.
6. Check delivery feasibility, willingness to adopt, switching costs, and likely competitive responses.
7. Write a positioning statement and a proof plan. Remove superiority or exclusivity language unsupported by evidence.

## Output

Audience and Scenario, competitive overlay, Outcome opportunity hypotheses, offer, positioning statement, proof points, objections, and validation plan.

## Stop condition

The position is understandable, deliverable, relevant to the audience, and accompanied by a test of its key demand assumptions.

## Critical distinction

A novel label, larger grid, or empty coordinate does not establish a blue-ocean market, legal exclusivity, or a defensible advantage.
