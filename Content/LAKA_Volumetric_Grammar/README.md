**Version:** 1.0-draft

## Start here

Read `LAKA_MASTER_GRAMMAR.md` for the full fifteen-section framework. For an applied run, copy `04_templates/RUN_LAKA.md`, fill in the context, and use the matching playbook in `02_operating_modes/`. Record your work with `04_templates/WORKSHEET.md`.

The three axes remain exactly as supplied: **five change levels × ten internal variables × fourteen meta-variables = 700 base coordinates**. A coordinate is a question or analysis slot—not a guaranteed distinct, feasible, novel, or valuable innovation.

## Two archive editions

`LAKA_Complete_Package.zip` includes all LAKA materials and the user-supplied source PDF. `LAKA_Framework_Only.zip` includes the same LAKA materials without the source PDF for a smaller download. Each ZIP contains its own accurate manifest.

## How to use the prompt library

Choose a Scenario and Actor. Establish the Baseline before proposing changes. Select a mode, then a focused subset of coordinates or the full 700-coordinate scan. Save each answer as an observation, assumption, hypothesis, proposed transformation, or documented not-applicable result. Combine compatible transformations into a complete system sentence and test the result.

`LAKA-C3-I07-M08` means **Structural Change × Feedback × Acceleration**. Codes and labels are defined in `05_machine_readable/axes.json`.

## What is original versus source-inspired

The user-defined axes are LAKA. The volumetric grammar, six-mode architecture, prompts, templates, schema, examples, and scoring rubric are proposed LAKA design work. The book provides inspiration through its own Actors, Desires, Scenarios, Outcomes, Elements, Alternatives, functional distinctions, inversion, and innovation-sequence approach. Its original Alternatives Grid is not the LAKA five-column change grid.

Portable references such as `[PI-11]` point to `08_sources/SOURCE_NOTES.md`, which gives PDF-page and printed-page locators. This avoids depending on conversation-only citation links.

## Status and limits

This package is a specification and working toolkit, not a deployed application, a proven universal grammar, or an empirically validated forecasting system. The state ladders are illustrative vocabularies, not calibrated numerical scales. Proposed opportunities require evidence and testing. An unfilled cell does not establish novelty or demand. A decoded explanation is not proof of intent or wrongdoing.
